# Appended to every scene. The two labels below map onto H3's last two sections,
# so keep them - without them everything is treated as diegetic sound.

[overall_soundscape]
  Diegetic sound only - what a microphone in the scene would pick up.
  Always present and always quiet: <the location's ambience, three or four
  specific sounds>. Never louder than the vocal.
  NO cartoon sound effects. No boings, no slide whistles, no comedy impacts on
  cuts.

[non_diegetic_music]
  The supplied song excerpt is the only music. Do not add, invent or extend any
  instrumentation - no score, no stings, no risers.
