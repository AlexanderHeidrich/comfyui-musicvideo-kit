# For hand-drawn 2D. A rostrum camera photographs flat artwork, so it can only
# slide across a painting, truck in on a cel, or hold. No 3D moves, no orbits,
# no parallax that a multiplane rig could not produce.

[v1] wide master, held
[Static shot] Locked off, wide, no camera move. Characters placed on a painted
background, whole location readable, composition centred and TV-safe.

[v2] lateral pan across the background
[Pan right] A slow, even lateral move across a long background painting at
constant speed, the way a rostrum camera slides over artwork. Foreground layer
drifts slightly faster than the background - multiplane, not 3D. No vertical
movement.

[v3] truck-in on a held cel
[Push in] A slow, even truck-in on the single most specific element - a face, a
hand, a point of contact. The drawing itself does not change during the move;
only the framing tightens. Ends locked.
