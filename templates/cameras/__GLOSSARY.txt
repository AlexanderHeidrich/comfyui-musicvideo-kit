Camera vocabulary that video models actually follow. Use the term, not the mood.

SHOT SIZE - how much of the subject is in frame
  extreme wide          the subject is small in a large space, geography reads
  wide / full           whole body, feet to head, setting still readable
  medium wide           knees up. The workhorse for two characters
  medium                waist up. Dialogue default
  medium close-up       chest up. Reaction default
  close-up              head and shoulders. One emotion, no context
  extreme close-up      one feature: an eye, a mouth, a hand, a texture
  insert                an object alone, no character. Cuts in anywhere

ANGLE - where the camera sits
  eye level             neutral, the subject meets the viewer
  low angle             camera below the eyeline, subject gains authority
  high angle            camera above, subject reads small or exposed
  overhead / top down   straight down. Graphic, shows arrangement
  worm's eye            on the ground looking up. Extreme low
  bird's eye            far above, the whole location as a pattern
  over the shoulder     behind one character, framing the other past them
  dutch angle           horizon tilted. Use once, on purpose
  profile               dead side-on. Flattens depth, reads as a statement
  three-quarter         between front and profile. Most natural for faces

MOVEMENT - what the camera does. One dominant move per clip
  [Static shot]         locked off. The strongest choice more often than not
  [Push in]             camera travels toward the subject, parallax changes
  [Pull out]            camera travels away, revealing context
  [Zoom in] / [Zoom out]  focal length only, no travel. A different feeling
  [Pan left] / [Pan right]   camera pivots on its axis
  [Truck left] / [Truck right]   whole camera slides sideways
  [Pedestal up] / [Pedestal down]  camera rises or drops, staying level
  [Tilt up] / [Tilt down]   camera pivots vertically
  [Tracking shot]       camera follows a moving subject, holding framing
  [Shake]               handheld instability. Rarely what you want

LENS FEEL - describe the result, not the hardware
  deep focus            everything sharp front to back
  shallow focus         subject sharp, background falls to soft colour
  rack focus            focus moves from one plane to another during the shot
  wide lens feel        near objects loom, edges stretch, space exaggerated
  long lens feel        background compressed and close, layers stack flat

WHAT NOT TO WRITE
  "cinematic", "epic", "beautiful", "masterpiece", "8k", "hyperrealistic",
  camera brands, film stocks, director names, lens model numbers, and any
  stack of three or more moves in one clip.
