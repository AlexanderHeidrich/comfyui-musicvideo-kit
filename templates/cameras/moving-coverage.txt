# One dominant move per variant. Use when the scene needs energy. Never stack
# these moves - each variant carries exactly one.

[v1] wide, slow reveal
[Pull out] Starts on the subject at medium wide and travels back, opening the
full location around them. Eye level, deep focus. The move is slow and constant
and ends locked.

[v2] medium, following
[Tracking shot] Camera holds the character at medium in the same part of the
frame while they move through the space. Eye level, slight shallow focus so the
background reads as passing colour.

[v3] close, pressing in
[Push in] Starts at medium close-up and travels toward the face, ending in
close-up. Parallax changes as it moves - this is a travelling camera, not a zoom.
