# Observational, as if someone is in the room with a camera on their shoulder.
# Use sparingly - H3's [Shake] is coarse, so the handheld feel is carried by the
# framing description more than by the command.

[v1] wide, found framing
[Static shot] Wide, eye level, slightly off-centre as if the operator settled
where they could stand. Framing is a little loose, headroom uneven, the horizon
not quite level. No move.

[v2] medium, reframing
[Pan right] Medium, eye level. The camera drifts to keep the subject in frame as
they shift, correcting a scene late, the way a person following the action does.
Small, unplanned adjustment only.

[v3] close, over the shoulder
[Static shot] Close, over the shoulder of the other character, the near shoulder
soft and out of focus in the foreground and filling a third of the frame. The
subject is sharp past it.
