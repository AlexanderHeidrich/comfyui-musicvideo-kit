# Held graphic compositions with one strong device per scene. The drama comes
# from angle and framing, not from movement.

[v1] wide, low and symmetrical
[Static shot] Wide, low angle from near ground level, the subject centred
against sky or ceiling with a lot of empty space above them. Held, no move.

[v2] profile, dead side-on
[Static shot] Medium close-up in flat profile, dead side-on, subject facing frame
left with the background compressed behind them like a painted flat. Held.

[v3] extreme close-up on one feature
[Static shot] Extreme close-up on a single feature - one eye, a mouth, a hand
tightening. Fills the frame. Nothing else is readable. Held.
