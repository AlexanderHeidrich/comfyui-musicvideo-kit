# Default set. Three locked-off setups on one moment - safest with H3, because
# nothing competes with the action. Same as templates/cameras.txt ships with.

[v1] wide master
[Static shot] Locked off, wide, no camera move. The full setting with the
character placed in it, deep focus, so the whole location reads at once.

[v2] alternative angle, medium
[Static shot] Locked off medium on a different axis - roughly ninety degrees off
the master, lower and closer. The character fills more of the frame and the
background falls soft behind. Same moment, seen from the other side of it.

[v3] close / detail
[Static shot] Locked off close on the single most specific thing in the action -
a face, a hand, a point of contact. Everything else reads as soft colour.
