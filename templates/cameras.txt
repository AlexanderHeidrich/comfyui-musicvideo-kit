# The three camera setups written for every scene. v1/v2/v3 are COVERAGE of one
# moment, not alternative scenes: the ACTION text is identical in all three and
# only this block changes, so they can be cut together inside the same scene and
# they share the one scene mp3.
# Format: [vN] label, then the block. Add v4 and it is generated too.

[v1] wide master
Locked off, wide, no camera move. The full setting with the character placed in
it, deep enough focus that the whole location reads at once.

[v2] alternative angle, medium
Locked off medium on a different axis - roughly ninety degrees off the master,
lower and closer. The character fills more of the frame and the painted
background falls soft behind. Same moment, seen from the other side of it.

[v3] close / detail
Locked off close on the single most specific thing in the action - a face, a
hand, a point of contact. Everything else reads as soft painted colour.
