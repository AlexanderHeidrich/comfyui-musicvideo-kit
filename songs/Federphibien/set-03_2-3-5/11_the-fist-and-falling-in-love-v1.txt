subject_definitions
<Subject 1> is THE FROG, shown in <Picture 1>: a common European pond frog, 8 cm nose to rump
and 10 cm sitting up - the length of a man's thumb, and he fits in a cupped human
hand. He sits squat and is wider than he is tall, with a big round pale cream
belly. Grey-green skin, not warty, with soft olive mottling. Large white eyes set
high and wide on top of the skull, each with a small black dot pupil, domed above
the skull line so they break his silhouette from every angle. A wide mouth drawn
as one long calm curve. Damp, with a soft pale highlight where the light catches
him. Introverted, hopelessly in love, entirely sincere.

<Subject 2> is THE HEN, shown in <Picture 2>: an ordinary plump farmyard hen, 45 cm to the top of
her comb and 50 cm beak to tail - four to five times the frog's height. Standing
beside her he reaches her hock and no higher, and she must fold her neck right to
the ground to bring her beak to him. She stands on two thin scaly yellow-orange
legs, body carried fairly upright in a soft pear shape, breast full and rounded,
the tail carried up and fanned into a short point. Warm tan and ochre plumage
throughout, the folded wing picked out in fine dark hatching rather than a
darker colour, and a pale cream breast and belly. Upright brick-red comb, small dark red wattle,
short orange-yellow beak, the same white eyes with dot pupils as the frog. No
eyelashes, no blush, no anthropomorphism. Noble, haughty, indifferent.

<Subject 3> is the pond and the yard around it, built from the separate element
studies in <Picture 3> - a sheet of parts on bare paper, not a view of the place,
so its layout is never reproduced. A calm flat pale green water surface, ripples as thin
concentric ink rings; lily pads outlined in dark ink with pale green centres;
tall cattails standing into frame at both edges as near-black silhouettes with
brown seed heads; a bank of dry mud cracked into plates; a band of tall pale
straw-coloured grass; a leaning square-mesh wire fence on rough leaning posts,
worn under into a gap a frog can pass; beyond it the coop, a long
grey-green weatherboard shed with a ridged roof; and on the horizon a low pale
hedge line with nothing built beyond it. The yard, the fence and the coop stand
on the far side of the water from the bank the frog hauls out on. Palette here: leaf green over pale
green-white water, sage and olive land, dusty liver brown, straw, cream, and a
high pale washed-out sky. The pond is 3 m across bank to bank, the farmyard
40 m, and it is 5 m from the far bank to the hen house. The fence and the coop roof are in every shot
that holds the far bank, at any distance, from any angle, including straight down.

detailed_description
Pen and watercolour on cold-press paper, in the manner of a children's picture
book. A fine, even dark brown ink line drawn with a pen and not a brush, holding
its weight all the way round, with dense short hatching strokes following the
direction of feather or form wherever something is shaded. Inside the line, loose
transparent washes that pool and grade softly and sometimes drift past it, the
tooth of the paper showing through every one of them. One soft pale highlight on
each rounded wet surface, never a hard white dot. A soft cast shadow under
anything standing on the ground. Warm muted palette: grey-green and olive, warm
tan and ochre, cream and bone, brick red only on a comb. Values sit in the
middle: nothing is pure white, nothing is pure black. Calm and unhurried, never
zany, never television cel animation and never vector. Limited animation on twos
with strong held poses.
Output clean and sharp: no film grain, video noise, VHS softness, scanlines,
chroma bleed, gate weave, dust, vignetting, bloom, lens effects or on-screen
text.

The scene is <Subject 3>, the pond and the farmyard around it: the near bank of dry cracked mud and pale straw grass, the flat pale green water beyond it, and across the water the yard, the leaning chicken-wire fence and the long coop. Bright summer daylight from high and left.

Screen geometry, the same in every shot at the pond: seen from the side, the frog's own bank is on the left, the water lies across the middle, and the far bank with the yard, the leaning fence and the coop is on the right. He sits and then lies on the dry cracked mud and straw grass of the left-hand bank, never out on the water, and the hen is small on the far bank by the coop, so the whole width of the water lies between the two of them.

[Shot 1] An extreme wide shot. The camera pushes in on him with large amplitude at slow speed, so that the far bank and the hen fall away behind him and the water opens out between them. The frog sits on the dry mud of his own bank and looks across the water at the hen, who is small on the far bank and goes on pecking without once looking up. He draws one fist back and holds it there.

[Shot 2] At 00:03.000, the shot cuts to: an extreme close-up square on the fist and the cracked mud under it. He brings it down on the dry cracked mud, and again, the second time weakly, and leaves it lying there.

[Shot 3] At 00:05.500, the shot cuts to: a wide shot from across the water, the whole width of it in front of him. He lets himself fall backwards onto his back in the straw grass, and the fist comes up and settles against his chest over his heart. He keeps his eyes on the sky.

[Shot 4] At 00:08.000, the shot cuts to: a close-up of his face from above. Up in the pale sky the hen's profile stands half-transparent and still, and one tear gathers and glitters at the corner of his eye.

overall_soundscape
A working summer farm, quiet and constant under everything: wet plops, crickets,
the low mutter of hens off frame, corrugated tin ticking in the heat, one distant
tractor that never arrives. Flies. No cartoon sound effects of any kind - no
boings, no slide whistles, no comedy impacts. Any croak or cluck is
recorded-real, never performed.
