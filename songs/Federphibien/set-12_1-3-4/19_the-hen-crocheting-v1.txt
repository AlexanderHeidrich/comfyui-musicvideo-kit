detailed_description
Hand-painted picture-book animation. Loose watercolour and gouache washes on
cold-press paper, the tooth of the paper showing through every wash; colour
pools and grades softly inside each shape and sometimes drifts past the line.
Over the paint a thin dry dark brown-black ink contour of clearly varying
weight, slightly ragged where the brush lifted, with short hatching strokes for
feathers and for the shaded side of a form. One soft pale highlight on each
rounded wet surface, never a hard white dot. Values sit in the middle: nothing
is pure white, nothing is pure black. Limited animation on twos with strong held
poses.
Output clean and sharp: no film grain, video noise, VHS softness, scanlines,
chroma bleed, gate weave, dust, vignetting, bloom, lens effects or on-screen
text.

The scene is a daydream and has no real place: the characters are on a plain warm cream ground that fades out at the edges, with nothing painted behind them.

[Shot 1] The camera zooms out with medium amplitude at slow speed, opening from the hen to the three young. The hen sits upright on a plain wooden chair, working a crochet hook with small steady movements. In front of her three small Federphibien sit on the ground, pushing soft toys shaped like tadpoles about between them. Nobody hurries and nobody looks up.
