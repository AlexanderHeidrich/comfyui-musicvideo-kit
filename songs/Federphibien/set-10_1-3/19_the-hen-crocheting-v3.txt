subject_definitions
<Subject 1> is THE FEDERPHIBIUM, shown in <Picture 1>, and it exists only inside a daydream: a
fat potbellied sage-green frog body sitting squat like a toad, about 12 cm to the
top of the comb, with a large pale cream belly and mottled olive patches. An
upright brick-red chicken comb standing off the top of its head, and a small red
wattle hanging under a short yellow-orange beak. Frog legs and frog feet, long toes ending in small yellow
claws, and two small useless tufts of cream-white feathers at the flanks where
wings would be. Large white eyes with small black dot pupils, set high
and wide enough that they break the silhouette from behind, where it reads as a
rounded mottled back with the comb standing clear above it and a cream feather
tuft out at either side.

<Subject 2> is THE HEN, shown in <Picture 2>: an ordinary plump farmyard hen, 45 cm to the top of
her comb and 50 cm beak to tail - four to five times the frog's height. Standing
beside her he reaches her hock and no higher, and she must fold her neck right to
the ground to bring her beak to him. She stands on two thin scaly yellow-orange
legs, body carried fairly upright in a soft pear shape, breast full and rounded,
the tail carried up and fanned into a short point. Warm tan and ochre plumage
throughout, the folded wing picked out in fine dark hatching rather than a
darker colour, and a pale cream breast and belly. Upright brick-red comb, small dark red wattle,
short orange-yellow beak, the same white eyes with dot pupils as the frog. No
eyelashes, no blush, no anthropomorphism. Noble, haughty, indifferent.

detailed_description
Pen and watercolour on cold-press paper, in the manner of a children's picture
book. A fine, even dark brown ink line drawn with a pen and not a brush, holding
its weight all the way round, with dense short hatching strokes following the
direction of feather or form wherever something is shaded. Inside the line, loose
transparent washes that pool and grade softly and sometimes drift past it, the
tooth of the paper showing through every one of them. One soft pale highlight on
each rounded wet surface, never a hard white dot. A soft cast shadow under
anything standing on the ground. Warm muted palette: grey-green and olive, warm
tan and ochre, cream and bone, brick red only on a comb. Values sit in the
middle: nothing is pure white, nothing is pure black. Calm and unhurried, never
zany, never television cel animation and never vector. Limited animation on twos
with strong held poses.
Output clean and sharp: no film grain, video noise, VHS softness, scanlines,
chroma bleed, gate weave, dust, vignetting, bloom, lens effects or on-screen
text.

The scene is a daydream seen through cloud, a background painting in which
nothing is a subject: the characters are on a soft warm cream ground with no
horizon and no floor line, and banks of white and pale grey-blue watercolour
cloud stand in from all four edges, drifting slowly inward and thinning to
clear cream in the middle of the frame where the action is. The cloud is
painted in the same wash as everything else, its edges feathered and left
without an ink contour. Behind the characters there is cloud and cream and
nothing else: no pond, no water, no fence, no coop, no hen house, no walls, no
floor and no furniture the action does not name. Light is flat and even and
comes from everywhere at once. Palette here: white, pale grey-blue, warm cream.

[Shot 1] The camera holds a static shot on the three young and their toys. The hen sits upright on a plain wooden chair, working a crochet hook with small steady movements. In front of her three small Federphibien sit on the ground, pushing soft toys shaped like tadpoles about between them. Nobody hurries and nobody looks up.

overall_soundscape
A working summer farm, quiet and constant under everything: wet plops, crickets,
the low mutter of hens off frame, corrugated tin ticking in the heat, one distant
tractor that never arrives. Flies. No cartoon sound effects of any kind - no
boings, no slide whistles, no comedy impacts. Any croak or cluck is
recorded-real, never performed.

non_diegetic_music

No singing and no voice: nobody speaks and nobody sings, and no character's
