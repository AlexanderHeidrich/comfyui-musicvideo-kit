subject_definitions
<Subject 1> is THE FEDERPHIBIUM, shown in <Picture 1>, and it exists only inside a daydream: a
fat potbellied sage-green frog body sitting squat like a toad, about 12 cm to the
top of the comb, with a large pale cream belly and mottled olive patches. An
upright brick-red chicken comb standing off the top of its head, and a small red
wattle hanging under a short yellow-orange beak. Frog legs and frog feet, long toes ending in small yellow
claws, and two small useless tufts of cream-white feathers at the flanks where
wings would be. Large white eyes with small black dot pupils, set high
and wide enough that they break the silhouette from behind, where it reads as a
rounded mottled back with the comb standing clear above it and a cream feather
tuft out at either side.

<Subject 2> is THE RESEARCHER, shown in <Picture 2>: the only human in the film, 180 cm tall -
eighteen times the frog, four times the hen, who reaches just below his knee.
Khaki-olive trousers, a pale shirt with the sleeves rolled to the elbow, a wide
flat-brimmed straw hat, brown leather shoes, a small green notebook and pencil,
and an old black folding plate camera at his chest on broad dark straps across
both shoulders, reading as a dark block, not a bag. His face is never readable:
either his back is to us, or the hat brim lies flat across it while he bends over
the notebook. <Picture 2> also holds a side view in which his profile can be seen - that
view is construction only and is never what a shot uses. He never reacts, never
speaks, and the animals never notice him.

<Subject 3> is the pond and the yard around it, built from the separate element
studies in <Picture 3> - a sheet of parts on bare paper, not a view of the place,
so its layout is never reproduced. A calm flat pale green water surface, ripples as thin
concentric ink rings; lily pads outlined in dark ink with pale green centres;
tall cattails standing into frame at both edges as near-black silhouettes with
brown seed heads; a bank of dry mud cracked into plates; a band of tall pale
straw-coloured grass; a leaning square-mesh wire fence on rough leaning posts,
worn under into a gap a frog can pass; beyond it to the right the coop, a long
grey-green weatherboard shed with a ridged roof; and on the horizon a low pale
hedge line with nothing built beyond it. Palette here: leaf green over pale
green-white water, sage and olive land, dusty liver brown, straw, cream, and a
high pale washed-out sky. The pond is 3 m across, the farmyard 40 m, and it is
5 m from the bank to the hen house. The fence and the coop roof are in every shot
that holds the far bank, at any distance, from any angle, including straight down.

detailed_description
Pen and watercolour on cold-press paper, in the manner of a children's picture
book. A fine, even dark brown ink line drawn with a pen and not a brush, holding
its weight all the way round, with dense short hatching strokes following the
direction of feather or form wherever something is shaded. Inside the line, loose
transparent washes that pool and grade softly and sometimes drift past it, the
tooth of the paper showing through every one of them. One soft pale highlight on
each rounded wet surface, never a hard white dot. A soft cast shadow under
anything standing on the ground. Warm muted palette: grey-green and olive, warm
tan and ochre, cream and bone, brick red only on a comb. Values sit in the
middle: nothing is pure white, nothing is pure black. Calm and unhurried, never
zany, never television cel animation and never vector. Limited animation on twos
with strong held poses.
Output clean and sharp: no film grain, video noise, VHS softness, scanlines,
chroma bleed, gate weave, dust, vignetting, bloom, lens effects or on-screen
text.

The scene is <Subject 3>, the pond and the farmyard around it: flat pale green water, the cracked mud bank, the leaning chicken-wire fence and the coop with its ridged tin roof beyond it. Bright summer daylight from high and left.

[Shot 1] The camera pushes in over the water from high above, angled down onto the water, at slow speed, the speed never changing from the first frame to the last. Five or six Federphibien drift on the flat green water, dead. Each lies back uppermost and low in the water, the mottled olive back and the soaked cream feather tufts just breaking the surface, the frog legs splayed out and hanging slack below with the toes trailing. The head has lolled over to one side, both eyes are shut - smooth closed lids over the domes high on the skull, no white and no black pupil anywhere - the brick-red comb has flopped flat and gone dark with water, and the tongue hangs out of the open beak and lies along the surface. Nothing about them moves under its own power: they turn slowly as the water carries them and drift apart, and thin ink rings spread from each body. At the far side of the pond a man sits on a folding chair on the bank with his back to us, not moving. He is soft and unclear at first and then settles.

[Shot 2] At 00:05.500, the shot cuts to: two of the bodies seen from straight above, backs uppermost on the flat water, the legs hanging down into the green and one tongue lying out across the surface.

overall_soundscape
A working summer farm, quiet and constant under everything: wet plops, crickets,
the low mutter of hens off frame, corrugated tin ticking in the heat, one distant
tractor that never arrives. Flies. No cartoon sound effects of any kind - no
boings, no slide whistles, no comedy impacts. Any croak or cluck is
recorded-real, never performed.

non_diegetic_music
No music of any kind. No song, no score, no stings, no risers, no instruments.
No singing and no voice: nobody speaks and nobody sings, and no character's
mouth forms words. Ambient sound only.
