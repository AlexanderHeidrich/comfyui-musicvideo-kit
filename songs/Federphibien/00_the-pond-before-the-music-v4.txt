A weathered enamel sign hangs from a leaning post at the near edge of frame and sways gently, its lettering worn away to nothing. Dragonflies criss-cross low over the water. Every few seconds a gust runs through the reeds and lays them over, then lets them stand again. Cattails stand up out of the water at both edges of frame as near-black silhouettes, and lily pads lie in loose clusters across it, each with its white glint. Along the top of the far bank runs the leaning chicken-wire fence on its rough posts, sagging between them, and behind the fence to the right the coop shows its ridged corrugated tin roof end-on. Beyond that the farmhouse and the cow barn sit far off, pale and flat with distance. At the far edge of the pond, small and easy to miss, a man already sits on a folding chair, bent right down over a notebook, the brim of his straw hat lying flat across his face so that nothing of the face is visible at all - only the crown of the hat and the shoulders under it. He does not move and nothing else happens.
