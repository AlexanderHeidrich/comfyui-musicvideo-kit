subject_definitions
<Subject 1> is THE FEDERPHIBIUM, shown in <Picture 1>, and it exists only inside a daydream: a
fat potbellied sage-green frog body sitting squat like a toad, about 12 cm to the
top of the comb, with a large pale cream belly and mottled olive patches. An
upright brick-red chicken comb on its head, a red two-lobed wattle under a small
yellow-orange beak. Frog legs and frog feet, long toes ending in small yellow
claws, and two small useless tufts of cream-white feathers at the flanks where
wings would be. Large white eyes with small black dot pupils.

<Picture 2> is a colour and construction board: read the palette, the line weight and the
way the wash grades inside the line off it. It is never a thing in the scene and
is never drawn.

<Subject 2> is THE RESEARCHER, shown in <Picture 3>: the only human in the film, 180 cm tall -
eighteen times the frog, four times the hen, who reaches just below his knee.
Khaki-olive trousers, a pale shirt with the sleeves rolled to the elbow, a wide
flat-brimmed straw hat, a small green notebook and pencil, and an old black
leather folding plate camera at his chest on a broad black strap over one
shoulder, reading as a dark block, not a bag. His face is never readable: either
his back is to us, or the hat brim lies flat across it while he bends over the
notebook. He never reacts, never speaks, and the animals never notice him.

<Picture 4> is the pond and the yard around it, a background painting in which nothing is
a subject. A calm flat pale green water surface skinned with duckweed, ripples as
thin concentric ink rings; lily pads with red-brown outlines, dark green centres
and one soft highlight each; cattails at both edges of frame as near-black
silhouettes; a bank of dry mud cracked into plates; a band of tall pale grass; a
leaning chicken-wire fence on rough posts, sagging and worn under into a gap a
frog can pass; behind it to the right the coop with its ridged corrugated tin
roof; far off the farmhouse and cow barn, pale with distance. Palette here: leaf
green over pale green-white water, sage and olive land, dusty liver brown, straw,
cream, a clear bright sky. The pond is 3 m across, the farmyard 40 m, and it is
5 m from the bank to the hen house. The fence and the tin roof are in every shot
that holds the far bank, at any distance, from any angle, including straight down.

detailed_description
Hand-painted picture-book animation. Loose watercolour and gouache washes on
cold-press paper, the tooth of the paper showing through every wash; colour
pools and grades softly inside each shape and sometimes drifts past the line.
Over the paint a thin dry dark brown-black ink contour of clearly varying
weight, slightly ragged where the brush lifted, with short hatching strokes for
feathers and for the shaded side of a form. One soft pale highlight on each
rounded wet surface, never a hard white dot. Values sit in the middle: nothing
is pure white, nothing is pure black. Limited animation on twos with strong held
poses.
Output clean and sharp: no film grain, video noise, VHS softness, scanlines,
chroma bleed, gate weave, dust, vignetting, bloom, lens effects or on-screen
text.

The scene is the pond and the farmyard around it, exactly as painted in <Picture 4>: flat pale green water, the cracked mud bank, the leaning chicken-wire fence and the coop with its ridged tin roof beyond it. Bright summer daylight from high and left.

[Shot 1] The camera travels sideways along the water with large amplitude at slow speed, then settles on the man. Several Federphibien float on the flat green water with their backs uppermost, not moving. The view travels along the water past them. At the end of the travel a man is sitting on a folding chair on the bank with his back to us. He is soft and unclear at first and then settles.

[Shot 2] At 00:05.500, the shot cuts to: the floating hybrids seen from straight above, their backs uppermost on the flat water.

overall_soundscape
A working summer farm, quiet and constant under everything: wet plops, crickets,
the low mutter of hens off frame, corrugated tin ticking in the heat, one distant
tractor that never arrives. Flies. No cartoon sound effects of any kind - no
boings, no slide whistles, no comedy impacts. Any croak or cluck is
recorded-real, never performed.
