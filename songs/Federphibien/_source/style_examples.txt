# Free notes on the look, distilled into style.txt. Not pasted into the prompt.

PICKED, and then corrected. The first take was the flat-cel grubby one -
Ren & Stimpy / Biber Brueder, brush line with tooth, hard-edged shadow shapes.
The reference images that were actually generated and approved are NOT that:
they are the European ink-and-watercolour school - a fine umber nib line with
transparent washes graded inside it, paper tooth showing through, desaturated
sage and straw, low contrast, scenery outlined in the same medium as the
characters. Two frame grabs set it: the pond seen past foreground reeds, and the
close pass over lily pads.
style.txt was rewritten to match the images on 2026-08-31. If the two ever
disagree again, the images win - they are what H3 is actually handed.
The tidy late-90s take survives as templates/styles/90s-cartoon-clean.txt.
