FEDERPHIBIEN - MIDJOURNEY PROMPTS FOR THE REFERENCE IMAGES
==========================================================================

This folder is the rougher variant - THIS IS THE ONE YOU PICKED.

Save each result into this folder under the filename in its heading, then run:
  ./mvkit refs <this song> && ./mvkit build <this song>

Generate them IN THIS ORDER. The frog and the hen are the Federphibium taken
apart, so make that one first and keep it on screen for the other two - that is
what makes the three look related.

THE LOOK, in one paragraph. One frame grab sets it: a pond surface seen from
just above, lily pads drawn with a bold clean BLACK outline, a flat green base
inside each softly airbrushed darker toward the rim, one hard white glint on
every pad, floating on pale near-white green water that is almost empty, with
the ripples drawn as thin concentric rings. That is the target: black ink over
flat-plus-airbrush paint, high-key and calm - not loose watercolour, not flat
hard-edged cel shadow shapes either. Palette dusty and cool around that fresh
green: sage, olive, dusty liver brown, straw, cream, a lilac-grey overcast sky,
red only on a comb. Depth by haze - the far hedge paler and flatter, near
foliage a near-black silhouette. Paper tooth stays faint, under the paint.

Add your own --v flag; the version changes too often to hard-code here.

LOADING THEM IN COMFYUI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Seven images plus the scene mp3 is eight of H3's twelve media inputs, so they all
fit. Fill the picture slots IN THIS ORDER and never leave an earlier one empty -
H3 numbers <Picture n> over the slots actually connected, so a gap shifts every
tag after it and the prompts stop matching:

  slot 1  01_char_federphibium     always
  slot 2  02_char_der-frosch       always
  slot 3  03_char_das-huhn         always
  slot 4  04_style_board           always
  slot 5  05_char_der-forscher     only scenes 19, 20, 43, 44
  slot 6  06_loc_pond              only the pond scenes
  slot 7  07_loc_henhouse-interior only scenes 23 to 38

Slots 1-4 are the constant core. Leaving 5-7 out does not move their tags.
Simplest workable setup: load all seven everywhere and accept the extra
references; then nothing can shift at all.
