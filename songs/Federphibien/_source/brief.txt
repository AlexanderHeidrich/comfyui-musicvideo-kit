# The short build (v4): the same shot as v1, said in under 7000 characters,
# which is the prompt length every published H3 guide states. bible.txt and
# style.txt stay the long form for v1-v3; this is their condensate.
# {S} becomes this scene's <Subject n>, {P} its <Picture n>. A [subject] block
# is only used in the scenes its character is actually in.

[style]
Hand-drawn 2D television animation: clean bold black ink outlines over flat
colour, softly airbrushed darker toward the rim of each form and where forms
overlap. No hard-edged cel shadow shapes, no watercolour bloom. One hard white
glint on every wet rounded surface. High-key - the black line is the only real
dark. A clear bright summer day, warm sunlight from one fixed direction high
and left, short soft warm shadows, clean blue sky with scattered high
fair-weather cloud, no overcast and no rain.
Palette: leaf green over pale green-white water, sage and olive land, dusty
liver brown, straw, cream. Red only on a comb and a wattle. Limited animation on twos
with strong held poses. Faint paper tooth under the paint.
Output clean and sharp: no film grain, video noise, VHS softness, scanlines,
chroma bleed, gate weave, dust, vignetting, bloom, lens effects or on-screen text.

[sound]
A working summer farm, quiet and constant under everything: wet plops, crickets,
the low mutter of hens off frame, corrugated tin ticking in the heat, one distant
tractor that never arrives. Flies. No cartoon sound effects of any kind - no
boings, no slide whistles, no comedy impacts. The frog's croaks and the hen's
clucks are recorded-real, never performed. Never louder than the vocal.

[music]
The supplied song excerpt is the only music. Do not invent, add or extend any
instrumentation - no score, no stings, no risers.

[subject der frosch]
{S} is THE FROG, shown in {P}: a common European pond frog, 8 cm nose to rump
and 10 cm sitting up - the length of a man's thumb, and he fits in a cupped human
hand. Grey-green skin, not warty, with soft olive mottling and a pale cream belly. Large white eyes set high and wide, each with a small black dot pupil.
He is always wet and always glistening, and a small dark puddle forms wherever he
sits. Introverted, hopelessly in love, entirely sincere.

[subject das huhn]
{S} is THE HEN, shown in {P}: an ordinary sandy-tan farmyard hen, 45 cm to the
top of her comb and 50 cm beak to tail - four to five times the frog's height.
Standing beside her he reaches her hock and no higher, and she must fold her neck
right to the ground to bring her beak to him. Red comb and wattle, the same white
eyes with dot pupils as the frog. No eyelashes, no blush, no anthropomorphism.
Noble, haughty, indifferent.

[subject federphibium]
{S} is THE FEDERPHIBIUM, shown in {P}, and it exists only inside a daydream: a
fat potbellied sage-green frog body sitting squat like a toad, about 12 cm to the
top of the comb, with a large pale cream belly and mottled olive patches. An
upright red chicken comb on its head, a red two-lobed wattle under a small
yellow-orange beak. Frog legs and frog feet, long toes ending in small yellow
claws, and two small useless tufts of cream-white feathers at the flanks where
wings would be. Large white eyes with small black dot pupils.

[subject der forscher]
{S} is THE RESEARCHER, shown in {P}: the only human in the film, 180 cm tall -
eighteen times the frog, four times the hen, who reaches just below his knee.
Khaki trousers, a plain shirt with the sleeves turned back, a straw hat, a green
notebook and pencil, and an old black leather folding plate camera at his front
on a broad black strap over one shoulder, reading as a dark block, not a bag.
He sits on a folding chair at the edge of the pond and draws what he sees. His
face is NEVER visible: either he is at the front of the frame with his back to
us and everything else beyond him, or he is far off and bent down over the
notebook with the hat brim flat across his face. He never reacts, never speaks,
and the animals never notice him.

[subject pond]
{P} is the pond, a background painting in which nothing is a subject. Out from
the water: a calm flat pale green wash skinned with duckweed, ripples as thin
concentric ink rings; lily pads with red-brown outlines, dark green centres and
one white glint each; cattails at both edges of frame as near-black silhouettes
with no interior detail; a bank of dry mud cracked into plates; a band of tall
pale grass; a leaning chicken-wire fence on rough posts, sagging, torn and worn
under into a gap a frog can pass; behind it to the right the coop with its ridged
corrugated tin roof; far off the farmhouse and cow barn, pale with distance. The pond is
3 m across, the farmyard 40 m, and it is 5 m from the bank to the hen house. The
fence and the tin roof are in every shot that holds the far bank, at any distance,
from any angle, including straight down.

[subject henhouse interior]
{P} is the hen house interior painting and nothing in it is a subject: tall walls
of green-painted vertical timber boarding framed by heavy warm-brown posts and
beams, one horizontal rail across the wall at a third of its height, an ochre
bare-earth floor, a big heap of grey-white down banked against the left wall with
loose feathers on the floor and drifting in the air, a straw nesting shelf high
under the roof with eggs on it, two black cables strung across with one bare bulb
hanging off a sagging one, and a doorway to the right in a brown timber frame
filled with flat blown-out daylight. Never corrugated tin inside. 3.5 m to the
ridge, so the wall runs far above the hen and the hen far above the frog.

[subject board]
{P} is a colour and construction board: read the palette, the line weight and the
way shading grades inside the line off it. It is never a thing in the scene and
is never drawn.
