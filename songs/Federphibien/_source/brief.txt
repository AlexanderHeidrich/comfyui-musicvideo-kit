# The only source of the prompts. {S} becomes this scene's <Subject n>, {P} its
# <Picture n>. A block is only used in the scenes it is actually in.
#
# [style] is in EVERY prompt, so it holds the look and nothing else. Anything
# tied to a place lives in that place's [location] block instead - the old
# [style] carried "pale green-white water" into every scene and is why a
# proposal written for the hen house kept happening in a pond.

[style]
Hand-painted picture-book animation. Loose watercolour and gouache washes on
cold-press paper, the tooth of the paper showing through every wash; colour
pools and grades softly inside each shape and sometimes drifts past the line.
Over the paint a thin dry dark brown-black ink contour of clearly varying
weight, slightly ragged where the brush lifted, with short hatching strokes for
feathers and for the shaded side of a form. One soft pale highlight on each
rounded wet surface, never a hard white dot. Values sit in the middle: nothing
is pure white, nothing is pure black. Limited animation on twos with strong held
poses.
Output clean and sharp: no film grain, video noise, VHS softness, scanlines,
chroma bleed, gate weave, dust, vignetting, bloom, lens effects or on-screen
text.

[sound]
A working summer farm, quiet and constant under everything: wet plops, crickets,
the low mutter of hens off frame, corrugated tin ticking in the heat, one distant
tractor that never arrives. Flies. No cartoon sound effects of any kind - no
boings, no slide whistles, no comedy impacts. Any croak or cluck is
recorded-real, never performed.

[music]
No music of any kind. No song, no score, no stings, no risers, no instruments.
No singing and no voice: nobody speaks and nobody sings, and no character's
mouth forms words. Ambient sound only.

[subject der frosch]
{S} is THE FROG, shown in {P}: a common European pond frog, 8 cm nose to rump
and 10 cm sitting up - the length of a man's thumb, and he fits in a cupped human
hand. He sits squat and is wider than he is tall, with a big round pale cream
belly. Grey-green skin, not warty, with soft olive mottling. Large white eyes set
high and wide on top of the skull, each with a small black dot pupil, domed above
the skull line so they break his silhouette from every angle. A wide mouth drawn
as one long calm curve. Damp, with a soft pale highlight where the light catches
him. Introverted, hopelessly in love, entirely sincere.

[subject das huhn]
{S} is THE HEN, shown in {P}: an ordinary plump farmyard hen, 45 cm to the top of
her comb and 50 cm beak to tail - four to five times the frog's height. Standing
beside her he reaches her hock and no higher, and she must fold her neck right to
the ground to bring her beak to him. She stands on two thin scaly yellow-orange
legs, body carried fairly upright in a soft pear shape, breast full and rounded,
the tail carried up and fanned into a short point. Warm tan and ochre plumage
throughout, the folded wing picked out in fine dark hatching rather than a
darker colour, and a pale cream breast and belly. Upright brick-red comb, small dark red wattle,
short orange-yellow beak, the same white eyes with dot pupils as the frog. No
eyelashes, no blush, no anthropomorphism. Noble, haughty, indifferent.

[subject federphibium]
{S} is THE FEDERPHIBIUM, shown in {P}, and it exists only inside a daydream: a
fat potbellied sage-green frog body sitting squat like a toad, about 12 cm to the
top of the comb, with a large pale cream belly and mottled olive patches. An
upright brick-red chicken comb standing off the top of its head, and a small red
wattle hanging under a short yellow-orange beak. Frog legs and frog feet, long toes ending in small yellow
claws, and two small useless tufts of cream-white feathers at the flanks where
wings would be. Large white eyes with small black dot pupils, set high
and wide enough that they break the silhouette from behind, where it reads as a
rounded mottled back with the comb standing clear above it and a cream feather
tuft out at either side.

[subject der forscher]
{S} is THE RESEARCHER, shown in {P}: the only human in the film, 180 cm tall -
eighteen times the frog, four times the hen, who reaches just below his knee.
Khaki-olive trousers, a pale shirt with the sleeves rolled to the elbow, a wide
flat-brimmed straw hat, brown leather shoes, a small green notebook and pencil,
and an old black folding plate camera at his chest on broad dark straps across
both shoulders, reading as a dark block, not a bag. His face is never readable:
either his back is to us, or the hat brim lies flat across it while he bends over
the notebook. {P} also holds a side view in which his profile can be seen - that
view is construction only and is never what a shot uses. He never reacts, never
speaks, and the animals never notice him.

[location pond]
{P} is the pond and the yard around it, a background painting in which nothing is
a subject. A calm flat pale green water surface, ripples as thin
concentric ink rings; lily pads outlined in dark ink with pale green centres;
tall cattails standing into frame at both edges as near-black silhouettes with
brown seed heads; a bank of dry mud cracked into plates; a band of tall pale
straw-coloured grass; a leaning square-mesh wire fence on rough leaning posts,
worn under into a gap a frog can pass; beyond it to the right the coop, a long
grey-green weatherboard shed with a ridged roof; and on the horizon a low pale
hedge line with nothing built beyond it. Palette here: leaf green over pale
green-white water, sage and olive land, dusty liver brown, straw, cream, and a
high pale washed-out sky. The pond is 3 m across, the farmyard 40 m, and it is
5 m from the bank to the hen house. The fence and the coop roof are in every shot
that holds the far bank, at any distance, from any angle, including straight down.

[location henhouse interior]
{P} is the hen house interior, a background painting in which nothing is a
subject: tall walls of green-painted vertical timber boarding framed by heavy
warm-brown posts and beams, one horizontal rail across the wall at a third of its
height, an ochre bare-earth floor, a big heap of grey-white down banked against
the left wall with loose feathers on the floor and drifting in the air, a straw
nesting shelf high under the roof with eggs on it, two black cables strung across
with one bare bulb hanging off a sagging one, and a doorway to the right in a
brown timber frame filled with flat blown-out daylight. Palette here: green
timber, warm brown, ochre earth, grey-white down. Never corrugated tin inside,
never water, never a pond. 3.5 m to the ridge, so the wall runs far above the hen
and the hen far above the frog.

[subject board]
{P} is a colour and construction board: read the palette, the line weight and the
way the wash grades inside the line off it. It is never a thing in the scene and
is never drawn.
